Install on Android (step by step)

  1. Tap “Android APK”. The installer file downloads to your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Use the notification or your Files app.
  3. Allow unknown sources. If Android asks, open Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps and enable it for your browser or file manager.
  4. Tap Install. Android may show a Play Protect warning; choose “Install anyway” only if you downloaded the file from this official site.
  5. Register in the app. Use your real name, contact number and address, and upload a photo of a valid ID.
  6. Wait for approval. A barangay admin verifies your ID. You are notified when your account becomes active.

Install on iPhone

  1. Tap “iOS app” to open the App Store listing.
  2. Tap Get and confirm with your Apple ID or Face ID.
  3. Open the app and register with the same details you would give the barangay office.

How it works

From sign-up to resolution in four steps

The same flow for every resident: register, get verified, report, and follow up.

  1. Sign up in the app

    Fill in your name, age, contact number and address, then choose whether you are registering as a resident.

  2. Admin approves your valid ID

    Upload a photo of any valid ID. A barangay admin checks it against the records and activates your account.

  3. Report complaints or emergencies

    File a complaint with details and a photo, or send an emergency request with your exact location.

  4. Track status and read responses

    Watch your case move to Ongoing, Processing or Resolved and read the barangay's written response.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything residents usually ask before installing the app.

Why does my account need admin approval?

Complaints, emergency requests and household records are official barangay transactions, so the office must know that each account belongs to a real resident of the barangay. After you register, a barangay admin reviews your valid ID and activates your account. You will be notified in the app once it is approved.

What counts as a valid ID?

Any government-issued or barangay-issued ID with your name and photo, for example:

  • Barangay ID or barangay clearance
  • Philippine National ID (PhilSys), passport, driver's licence, UMID, SSS or PhilHealth ID
  • Voter's ID or voter's certification
  • Student ID (for students) or senior citizen / PWD ID

Take a clear photo in good lighting, with all four corners visible. Blurred or edited photos are declined and you will need to submit again.

What counts as an emergency?

Use the emergency request for situations that need immediate action from responders: fire, medical emergencies, accidents, flooding, fallen trees or live wires, and threats to safety.

For concerns that can wait for office hours — garbage collection, drainage, noise, stray animals or neighbour disputes — file a complaint instead, so responders stay free for real emergencies. Always dial 911 for life-threatening situations.

Who can see my reports and personal information?

Your complaint and emergency records are visible only to you, the assigned barangay officers and the barangay administrators. Other residents cannot browse your reports. Your password is stored securely, and only the information needed for barangay services (name, address, contact number, valid ID) is kept on file.

Announcements, published daily operations and hotlines are public — they contain no personal data.

Is the app free? Do I need mobile data?

Yes, the app is completely free for residents of the barangay. You need an internet connection (mobile data or Wi-Fi) to register, submit reports and load the latest announcements.
