Community Partner Requirements:

The community partner, Barangay San Jose Annex Area 6, Rodriguez, Rizal, requires a centralized Barangay Management Information System that will efficiently manage resident records, track resident complaints and emergency rescue requests, monitor daily barangay operations, and generate reports for faster, organized, and transparent public service.

Specifically, the barangay requires: (1) resident registration with valid ID verification and admin approval to ensure only legitimate Area 6 residents can access the system; (2) complaint tracking for non-emergency concerns with category, purok/sitio, address, details, and photo evidence, with status monitoring from Pending to Resolved; (3) emergency rescue service documentation for incidents requiring immediate response with emergency type, location, details, and photo evidence directed to authorized responders; (4) daily operations monitoring including community safety alerts, evacuation center readiness, personnel on patrol, and tanod operations log; (5) announcements and community information dissemination; (6) report history with privacy protection where only the resident and authorized responders can view records; and (7) notifications and profile management for continuous communication between residents and barangay officials. The system transitions the barangay from manual paper-based logging to a centralized digital platform to prevent delays, misplaced records, and data loss.
