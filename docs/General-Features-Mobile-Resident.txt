General Features

Barangay San Jose Annex Area 6 Emergency & Rescue MIS: Developing a Mobile Resident Application-Based Barangay Management Information System for Barangay San Jose Annex Area 6, Rodriguez, Rizal, specifically, it aims to:

3. Develop a mobile application with different features for:

a. User Access
i. Login Panel - Type in registered username or email and password. Only verified resident accounts approved by the admin can access the mobile application. Error prompts appear for invalid credentials and pending accounts.
ii. Register Panel - Register as an Area 6 resident by filling in full name, username, email, age, contact number, home address, valid ID type, and uploading a photo of valid ID for verification, as well as agreeing to data privacy before continuing. New accounts remain pending until approved by the admin.
iii. Forgot Password Panel - Recovery for forgotten credentials through verified email, allowing password changing for account security measures.

b. Home / Dashboard
i. Home - Viewing latest announcements, today's daily operations, recent complaints, and recent emergency requests in one overview panel, with quick navigation to details.
ii. Search and Refresh - Searching announcements and operations by keyword, with pull-to-refresh and pagination for updated community information.

c. Daily Operations
i. Community Safety - Displaying no active alerts or early warning status, and number of evacuation centers ready within the Area 6 evacuation network.
ii. Operations Summary - Displaying total number of personnel on patrol, active operations, and completed operations for the day.
iii. Operations Log - Area 6 Tanod - Viewing the list of daily operations such as Traffic Flow and Alley Clearing, including operation ID, date, shift schedule, description of duties performed, and officer-in-charge, with completed status.

d. Emergency Contacts
i. Need Immediate Help - A welcoming advisory panel instructing residents to call the nearest response unit below, and to call 911 for life-threatening emergencies.
ii. Call Directory - Displaying Barangay Ops Center contact cards with office telephone number and mobile number for immediate calling.
iii. Safety Centers - Exhibiting evacuation centers such as San Jose Annex Covered Court, including complete address and ready status, with early warning notice when Area 6 is under normal monitoring.

e. File a Report - Emergency
i. Emergency Tab - For incidents requiring immediate response, where the report goes directly to authorized Area 6 responders.
ii. Emergency Type - Selecting the type of emergency such as Fire, Medical, Crime, Flood, Accident, and Missing Person.
iii. Purok / Sitio and Specific Address - Selecting purok or sitio and typing block, lot, and street for exact location of the incident.
iv. Complaint Details - Describing the emergency situation clearly, including what happened and who is affected.
v. Photo Evidence - Uploading up to five photos as proof or supporting evidence of the reported emergency before submitting.

f. File a Report - Complaint
i. Complaint Tab - For non-emergency community concerns, where the complaint will be reviewed by the Resident Complaints and Grievance Desk.
ii. Complaint Category - Selecting a suggested category or typing the resident's own complaint type such as Place Complaint, Person Complaint, and Noise/Cleanliness.
iii. Purok / Sitio and Specific Address - Selecting purok or sitio and typing block, lot, and street for exact location of the complaint.
iv. Complaint Details - Describing the concern, when it happened, and who is affected for proper evaluation by barangay staff.
v. Photo Evidence - Uploading up to five photos as supporting evidence, then filing the resident complaint for admin review and response.

g. Report History
i. My Reports Overview - Displaying the total number of filed reports, active reports, and reports under review, with privacy notice that only the resident and authorized Area 6 responders can view these records.
ii. Filter Tabs - Viewing reports by All, Emergency, and Complaints, with search by report ID, subject, and location, and filter by status.
iii. Report List - Viewing each report's reference number, type, subject such as Road Obstruction, complete address, date filed, and status such as Under Review, with access to full details, status timeline, and admin response.

h. Community / Announcements
i. Announcements - Viewing published barangay announcements and community posts, including title, date, description, and attached images.
ii. Announcement Details - Opening a selected announcement to read full content and view supporting photos.

i. Notifications and Updates
i. Notifications - For informing residents of complaint status updates, emergency request updates, new announcements, and new daily operations.

j. Profile and Settings
i. Account Information - Where the user can update profile such as full name, email, contact number, age, home address, and profile photo.
ii. Change Password - Changing password using current password and confirming new password for account protection.
iii. About - Displaying barangay mission, vision, hotlines, and office information.
iv. Logout - When the user is done using the app, safely signing out of the resident account.

k. Accessibility and Usability
i. User-friendly Interface - For easy access to barangay services and information through bottom tab navigation for Operations, Emergency, Report, and History. Integration with the admin and website applications for synchronized data management.
